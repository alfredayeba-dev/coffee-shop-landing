How we pour at the bar

The same recipe we use behind the counter — for 300g water to 20g coffee, medium-fine grind.

0:00

Bloom

Pour 40g water, just off the boil. Let it sit — you'll see it puff up as the coffee releases CO₂.

0:30

First pour

Pour to 150g in slow concentric circles, working from the center out.

1:15

Second pour

Top up to 220g. Keep the bed of coffee flat and evenly saturated.

1:45

Final pour

Bring it up to 300g total, then let it draw down completely on its own.

3:00

Serve

Give it a swirl, pour, and drink it while it's still finding its shape.
